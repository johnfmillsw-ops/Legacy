This assessment examines six distinct areas of cognitive performance. Your results can contribute to a more comprehensive representation of your identity.

01

Fluid Reasoning

Pattern recognition and logical inference.

02

Visual-Spatial Ability

Mental rotation and spatial relationships.

03

Verbal Comprehension

Language, concepts and relationships.

04

Quantitative Reasoning

Numerical patterns and mathematical logic.

05

Working Memory

Retention and manipulation of information.

06

Processing Speed

Speed and accuracy of visual discrimination.

Before beginning

  • Allow approximately 60 minutes.
  • Use a quiet environment without distractions.
  • Do not use calculators, notes or outside assistance.
  • Keep the assessment window open and focused.
  • Memory exercises have controlled viewing periods.
  • Processing-speed sections have strict time limits.
  • Answers cannot be changed after submission.

Legacy's cognitive assessment is experimental. Numerical estimates use a theoretical scoring model and are not clinically validated or population-normed IQ scores.
